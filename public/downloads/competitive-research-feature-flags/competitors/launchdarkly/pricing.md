# LaunchDarkly Pricing

*Accessed 2026-02-27. Pricing changes frequently; verify directly at [launchdarkly.com/pricing](https://launchdarkly.com/pricing/).*

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Key Limits |
|---|---|---|
| **Developer** (Free) | $0 | 1 project, 3 environments, limited features |
| **Foundation** | $10/service connection/mo (annual) or $12/mo (monthly) | Multiple projects and environments |
| **Enterprise** | Custom (contact sales) | Advanced RBAC, custom roles, SSO, audit log |
| **Guardian** | Custom (contact sales) | All Enterprise features + release monitoring/guardrails |

## Typical Contract Range
$20,000 to $120,000+ per year depending on team size, flag volume, and customization ([Vendr](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/launchdarkly), [Spendflo](https://www.spendflo.com/blog/launchdarkly-pricing-guide), accessed 2026-02-27).

## Pricing Model
Per service connection (per seat). Enterprise and Guardian plans require sales consultation. No public pricing for enterprise tier.

## Notes
- Foundation plan is the entry point for teams; Developer is essentially a sandbox
- Enterprise pricing is opaque and negotiated -- typical for the category leader
- Financial services customers likely need Enterprise or Guardian for compliance features (audit log, RBAC, change approvals)
- No free trial mentioned for paid plans; Developer plan serves as a trial equivalent
