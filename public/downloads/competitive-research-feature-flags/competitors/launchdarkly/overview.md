# LaunchDarkly

## Company Overview
- **Website:** [https://launchdarkly.com](https://launchdarkly.com)
- **Founded:** 2014
- **Headquarters:** Oakland, California, USA
- **Funding/Revenue:** Approaching $200M ARR as of early 2026 ([Vendr](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/launchdarkly), accessed 2026-02-27)
- **Employee count:** Not precisely public; serves 5,500+ organizations including 37 of the Fortune 100 ([LaunchDarkly pricing page](https://launchdarkly.com/pricing/), accessed 2026-02-27)
- **Key leadership:** New CTO and CFO added January 2026

## Positioning
LaunchDarkly positions itself as the enterprise feature management platform, now expanding into "AI-ready software delivery infrastructure." They describe themselves as the market leader in feature management, used by development teams to control the entire feature lifecycle ([LaunchDarkly homepage](https://launchdarkly.com), accessed 2026-02-27).

## Target Market
Enterprise and mid-market companies across all industries. Specific vertical solutions for financial services, healthcare, and government. Strong presence in Fortune 100 companies.

## Product Summary
Full-featured feature management platform offering feature flags, progressive rollouts, experimentation/A/B testing, and release management. Handles trillions of flag evaluations daily with sub-millisecond latency. Cloud-hosted SaaS with US and EU regional instances. No self-hosted option.

## Canadian Banking Suitability
**Strengths:**
- SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, FedRAMP, HIPAA certified -- strong compliance posture
- Specific financial services solution page with RBAC, audit logging, change approval workflows, ServiceNow integration ([LaunchDarkly Financial Services](https://launchdarkly.com/solutions/financial-services/), accessed 2026-02-27)
- Largest market presence and most mature platform

**Concerns:**
- No Canadian data residency region (US and EU only as of Feb 2026) -- this is a significant gap for Canadian banks subject to PIPEDA and OSFI guidelines
- No self-hosted option -- all data must traverse LaunchDarkly's cloud infrastructure
- Premium pricing may be difficult to justify for smaller institutions
