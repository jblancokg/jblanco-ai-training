## Positioning
CloudBees is primarily a CI/CD and software delivery platform (built on Jenkins heritage) that includes feature management as one capability within its broader platform. Feature management is positioned as part of "CloudBees Unify" -- their software delivery management suite ([CloudBees Feature Management](https://www.cloudbees.com/capabilities/feature-management), accessed 2026-02-27).

## Target Market
Large enterprises already using CloudBees for CI/CD and software delivery. Organizations that want feature flags tightly integrated with their existing CloudBees pipeline.

## Product Summary
Feature flag management within a broader software delivery platform. Offers feature flags, remote configuration, and compliance features. Distinguishes between client-side (MAU-based billing) and server-side (unlimited) flags. Includes built-in OPA (Open Policy Agent) compliance policies for SOC 2, PCI, FedRAMP, and ISO 27001 ([CloudBees Compliance Features](https://www.cloudbees.com/capabilities/compliance/features), accessed 2026-02-27).

## Recent Developments
- Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA) compliance support for financial services
- CloudBees Unify platform with integrated compliance monitoring
- Community Edition available for free evaluation

## Canadian Banking Suitability
**Strengths:**
- SOC 2 and ISO 27001 certified (CloudBees ISMS and CloudBees Unify) ([CloudBees Trust Center](https://www.cloudbees.com/company/trust-center), accessed 2026-02-27)
- Built-in OPA compliance policies for SOC 2, PCI, FedRAMP, ISO 27001
- Auditor-dedicated compliance view
- DORA compliance support relevant to financial services
- Established enterprise vendor with long track record

**Concerns:**
- Feature management is not the core product -- it is a module within a larger platform
- Pricing is opaque and custom
- Smaller feature flag community and mindshare compared to LaunchDarkly or open-source tools
- Self-hosted deployment details not clearly documented for feature management specifically
- May require buying into the broader CloudBees platform
