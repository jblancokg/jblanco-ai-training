# CloudBees Feature Management Pricing

*Accessed 2026-02-27. Verify at [cloudbees.com/products/feature-management/pricing](https://www.cloudbees.com/products/feature-management/pricing).*

## Pricing Model
- Client-side feature flags: Billed per Monthly Active Users (MAUs)
- Server-side feature flags: Unlimited flags, no MAU charges
- Custom pricing -- requires sales consultation

Source: ([CloudBees FM Pricing](https://www.cloudbees.com/products/feature-management/pricing), accessed 2026-02-27)

## Plans
- **Community Edition:** Free, enterprise-grade feature flag management with limited scale ([CloudBees blog](https://www.cloudbees.com/blog/cloudbees-feature-management-community-edition-has-arrived), accessed 2026-02-27)
- **Paid plans:** Custom pricing, monthly or yearly billing
- **14-day free trial** available for full-featured version

## Notes
- Pricing is entirely opaque; no public pricing tiers
- MAU-based model for client-side can be unpredictable for high-traffic banking applications
- Server-side unlimited is attractive for backend microservices
- Likely most cost-effective if already using CloudBees for CI/CD (bundled pricing)
- Without existing CloudBees relationship, likely not competitive on price alone
