# Statsig Pricing

*Accessed 2026-02-27. Verify at [statsig.com/pricing](https://www.statsig.com/pricing).*

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Key Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Developer** (Free) | $0 | 2M metered events/month, feature gates + experiments + analytics |
| **Pro** | Usage-based | 5M free events/month, then per-event pricing |
| **Enterprise** | Custom annual | Flexible packaging, dedicated support |

Source: ([Statsig pricing](https://www.statsig.com/pricing), [G2 pricing](https://www.g2.com/products/statsig/pricing), accessed 2026-02-27)

## Pricing Model
Usage-based (metered events). Not per-seat. Statsig claims to be the only tool providing free feature flags at all scale levels for self-service companies ([Statsig comparison](https://www.statsig.com/comparison/best-free-feature-flagging-tools), accessed 2026-02-27).

## Notes
- Usage-based model is potentially very cost-effective for smaller teams
- No per-seat charges -- everyone on the team gets access
- Can become expensive at high event volumes
- Enterprise pricing requires sales engagement
- Free tier is generous (2M events/month)
