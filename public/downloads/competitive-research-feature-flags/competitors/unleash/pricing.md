# Unleash Pricing

*Accessed 2026-02-27. Verify at [getunleash.io/pricing](https://www.getunleash.io/pricing).*

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Key Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Open Source** (Self-hosted) | Free | Core feature flags, unlimited flags, community support |
| **Enterprise** (Cloud or Self-hosted) | $75/seat/month | 5-seat minimum ($375/month min). 14-day free trial. |

Source: ([Unleash pricing](https://www.getunleash.io/pricing), [G2 pricing](https://www.g2.com/products/unleash/pricing), accessed 2026-02-27)

## Pricing Model
Per-seat pricing for Enterprise. Open source is free. Simple two-tier model.

## Notes
- Simple, transparent pricing compared to competitors
- Enterprise is available both cloud-hosted and self-hosted at the same price point
- $75/seat/month with 5-seat minimum means minimum annual cost of ~$4,500
- No long-term commitment required; monthly billing available
- 14-day free trial with no credit card required
- For a team of 20, annual cost would be ~$18,000 -- significantly less than LaunchDarkly's typical enterprise contracts
- Open source edition is fully functional for basic feature flagging but lacks RBAC, change requests, and other compliance features critical for banking
