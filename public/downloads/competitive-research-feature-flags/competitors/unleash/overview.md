## Positioning
Unleash positions itself as "the leading open source feature management platform" built for developers, emphasizing deployment flexibility and core feature flagging without unnecessary complexity ([Unleash homepage](https://www.getunleash.io/), accessed 2026-02-27).

## Target Market
Developer teams that want open-source feature flags with strong self-hosting capabilities. Appeals to organizations that prioritize control over their infrastructure and want a focused feature flag tool without bundled experimentation or analytics.

## Product Summary
Open-source feature management platform with cloud-hosted and self-hosted options. Focuses on core feature flagging capabilities: gradual rollouts, kill switches, user segmentation, and activation strategies. Enterprise edition adds compliance features like RBAC, change requests, and audit logging. Known for its strong documentation on compliance use cases ([Unleash compliance docs](https://docs.getunleash.io/privacy-and-compliance/compliance-overview), accessed 2026-02-27).
