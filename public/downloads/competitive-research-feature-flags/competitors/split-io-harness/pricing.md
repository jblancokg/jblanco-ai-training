# Split.io (Harness FME) Pricing

*Accessed 2026-02-27. Verify at [split.io](https://www.split.io/) or [harness.io/pricing](https://www.harness.io/pricing).*

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Key Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Developer** (Free) | $0 | Up to 10 users |
| **Team** | $33/user/month | Core feature management |
| **Business** | $60/user/month | Advanced experimentation and analytics |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Full platform access, custom terms |

Source: ([Dimmo review](https://www.dimmo.ai/products/Split.io), [MarketingToolPro review](https://marketingtoolpro.com/2025/07/split-io-review/), accessed 2026-02-27)

## Pricing Model
Per-user (seat-based). The Harness platform also offers separate pricing for CI/CD and other modules, which may complicate total cost of ownership.

## Notes
- Pricing is in transition due to the Harness acquisition; may change as integration progresses
- Harness paid plans start at $100/month for the broader platform ([Harness pricing](https://www.harness.io/pricing), accessed 2026-02-27)
- Free tier is generous at 10 users
- Enterprise pricing requires sales engagement
