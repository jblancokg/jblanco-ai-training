## Positioning
Split.io, now branded as Harness FME (Feature Management & Experimentation), positions itself as a data-driven feature delivery platform. The acquisition by Harness integrates feature flags into a broader CI/CD and software delivery platform ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/05/29/harness-snags-split-io-as-it-goes-all-in-on-feature-flags-and-experiments/), accessed 2026-02-27).

## Target Market
Mid-market to enterprise engineering teams that want feature flags tightly integrated with CI/CD pipelines and experimentation. The Harness platform appeals to organizations already using or evaluating Harness for CI/CD.

## Product Summary
Feature management platform emphasizing experimentation and data-driven delivery. Every feature rollout can be treated as a controlled experiment with built-in statistical analysis. Now part of the broader Harness software delivery platform alongside CI/CD, cloud cost management, and security testing ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2024/05/29/harness-acquires-split-software-enable-feature-flag-experimentation-ci-cd-workflows/), accessed 2026-02-27).
