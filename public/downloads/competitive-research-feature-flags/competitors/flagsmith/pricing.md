# Flagsmith Pricing

*Accessed 2026-02-27. Verify at [flagsmith.com/pricing](https://www.flagsmith.com/pricing).*

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Key Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Open Source** (Self-hosted) | Free | No usage limits, full source code, BSD-3-Clause license |
| **Cloud Free** | $0/month | 50,000 requests/month, 1 user, 1 project |
| **Start-Up** | $45/month | Up to 1M requests/month, 3 users |
| **Enterprise** (Cloud or Self-hosted) | Custom | Dedicated support, SLA, advanced security features |

Source: ([Flagsmith pricing](https://www.flagsmith.com/pricing), [G2 pricing](https://www.g2.com/products/flagsmith/pricing), accessed 2026-02-27)

## Pricing Model
Usage-based (requests per month) for cloud plans. Self-hosted open source is completely free. Enterprise pricing is custom and requires sales contact.

## Notes
- The free self-hosted option with no usage limits is unique in the market and very attractive for cost-sensitive banks
- Cloud free tier is limited but useful for evaluation
- Enterprise self-hosted adds commercial support, SLA, and advanced features on top of the open source core
- For Canadian banks, the self-hosted free option means the tool cost is zero -- the cost is your infrastructure and ops team time
