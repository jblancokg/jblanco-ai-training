# DevCycle

## Company Overview
- **Website:** [https://devcycle.com](https://devcycle.com)
- **Founded:** 2011 as Taplytics; rebranded to DevCycle
- **Headquarters:** Toronto, Ontario, Canada ([DevCycle About Us](https://devcycle.com/company/about-us), accessed 2026-02-27)
- **Funding/Revenue:** Y Combinator-backed ([Y Combinator profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/devcycle), accessed 2026-02-27)
- **Employee count:** ~55 employees ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/61363-45), accessed 2026-02-27)

## Positioning
DevCycle positions itself as "the first and only OpenFeature-native feature management platform," built on edge architecture for fast, low-latency performance with enterprise-grade security and compliance ([DevCycle homepage](https://devcycle.com/), accessed 2026-02-27).

## Target Market
Developer teams that value performance, modern standards (OpenFeature), and usage-based pricing. Being Canadian-headquartered could be appealing to Canadian organizations with data sovereignty concerns.

## Product Summary
Feature flag management platform built on edge architecture for low-latency performance. Differentiates through OpenFeature-native implementation (the emerging open standard for feature flags) and usage-based pricing without per-seat charges. Every plan includes all integrations. Originally started as Taplytics (A/B testing for mobile), pivoted to developer-focused feature management ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/taplytics-launches-devcycle-an-industry-first-feature-management-suite-for-product-engineering-teams-301503678.html), accessed 2026-02-27).

## Recent Developments
