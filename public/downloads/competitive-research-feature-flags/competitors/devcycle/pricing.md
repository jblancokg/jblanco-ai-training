# DevCycle Pricing

*Accessed 2026-02-27. Verify at [devcycle.com/pricing](https://devcycle.com/pricing).*

## Pricing Model
Usage-based pricing tied to Monthly Active Users (MAUs) and cloud config requests. Not per-seat -- all team members get access on every plan. All integrations included on every plan ([DevCycle pricing](https://devcycle.com/pricing), accessed 2026-02-27).

## Plans
Specific tier names and prices were not detailed in available search results. DevCycle offers multiple tiers with the usage-based model described above.

Source: ([DevCycle pricing](https://devcycle.com/pricing), [G2 pricing](https://www.g2.com/products/devcycle/pricing), [Capterra Canada](https://www.capterra.ca/software/1026687/devcycle), accessed 2026-02-27)

## Notes
- Usage-based (MAU + requests) rather than per-seat is attractive for larger teams
- All integrations included on all plans reduces surprise costs
- Specific pricing tiers should be verified directly on the pricing page
- For Canadian banks, worth engaging sales to discuss data residency and hosting location
